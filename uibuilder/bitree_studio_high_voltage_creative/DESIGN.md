## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an electric, high-octane creative studio aesthetic calibrated for forward-thinking brand partners, digital product teams, and progressive agencies. It reconciles two opposing forces: ultra-saturated, hyper-vivid spectrums—drawn directly from the logo's signature green-to-cyan-to-amber radial luminosity—paired with an immaculate, museum-grade pure white canvas (`#FFFFFF`).

The visual movement fuses **Hyper-Color Maximalism** with **Swiss Structural Precision**. Interfaces feel crisp, airy, and razor-sharp, yet radiate youthful digital vitality through vivid gradient accents, multi-spectral stroke borders, and chromatic neon shadows. The emotional reaction is immediate optimism, creative capability, and polished technological craft.

## Colors

The palette embraces chromatic dynamism while maintaining strict WCAG AA/AAA legibility against pure white backgrounds.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (Bitree Electric Lime)**: `#10E868` (Surface accents, interactive glows, primary actions with dark typography)
- **Secondary (Cyan-Cobalt)**: `#0284C7` (Interactive links, tech tags, deep blue gradient anchor)
- **Tertiary (Neon Coral-Rose)**: `#F43F5E` (High-visibility highlights, badge counters, expressive accents)
- **Neutral Core**: `#0F172A` (Rich slate black for primary typography, structural lines, and heavy display headers)

### Spectrum Extension & Accent Gradients
- **Golden Aura**: `#FACC15` (Used in conjunction with `#10E868` for sunlit energy gradients)
- **Deep Violet**: `#7C3AED` (Used as a complementary spectrum anchor for multi-stop creative gradients)
- **Canvas Base**: `#FFFFFF` (Strict, pristine white canvas with zero muddy grey cast)
- **Surface Elevation**: `#F8FAFC` (Secondary card fills), `#F1F5F9` (Subtle dividers)

### Gradient Recipes
- `gradient-bitree-signature`: `linear-gradient(135deg, #10E868 0%, #06B6D4 50%, #FACC15 100%)`
- `gradient-electric-strike`: `linear-gradient(90deg, #10E868 0%, #0284C7 100%)`
- `gradient-sunset-pulse`: `linear-gradient(135deg, #F43F5E 0%, #FACC15 100%)`

## Typography

Typography establishes an architectural, high-energy rhythm. 

- **Display & Headings**: `Space Grotesk` imparts a contemporary, progressive tech posture with subtle idiosyncratic geometric cuts that echo creative agency boldness. Display headlines should utilize tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for graphic punch.
- **Body Copy**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` delivers fluid readability, wide apertures, and pristine geometric legibility across UI components, dashboards, and editorial paragraphs.
- **Micro-labels & Badges**: Small caps and uppercase variants set in `Space Grotesk` with loose tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) add visual poise and modern agency polish.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture balances high visual density inside modules with generous, breathable margins that allow pure white canvases to frame energetic elements.

- **Grid Framework**: Standard 12-column fluid grid system across desktop (1280px+ max-width 1440px), condensing to 8 columns on tablet (768px - 1024px), and 4 columns on mobile (<768px).
- **Rhythm**: Rooted in an 8px atomic unit, supporting predictable component composition and structured visual cadences.
- **Canvas Breathing Room**: Large outer section margins (`margin-section: 5rem` to `7rem` on desktop) prevent neon gradients and high-contrast components from visual crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids muddy, generic drop shadows. Instead, it relies on three bespoke depth treatments:

1. **Chromatic Ambient Glows**: Cards and buttons hover on radiant, low-opacity colored auras matching their accent hues (e.g., `0px 12px 32px rgba(16, 232, 104, 0.18)` or `0px 10px 28px rgba(2, 132, 197, 0.16)`).
2. **Double-Border Stratification**: Card containers use an ultra-thin 1px translucent border (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`) with subtle gradient corner highlights or vivid multi-color active strokes.
3. **Pure Frosted Layers (Daylight Glass)**: Floating navigational bars and popovers use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a crisp `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` top bevel line.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy is unified around a modern `0.5rem` (`8px`) base radius, scaling to `1rem` (`16px`) for cards and containers.

- **Primary Containers & Cards**: `rounded-lg` (`16px` / `1rem`) creates an approachable, modern tech footprint.
- **Buttons, Badges & Interactive Chips**: Retain playful flexibility. Category pills and status tags utilize full capsule pill bounds (`9999px`), while functional inputs and buttons use `8px` to `12px` geometries.
- **Graphic Motifs**: Geometric circle cutouts, glowing radial badges, and rounded rectangular frames referencing the fluid circular motion of the studio logo.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Electric**: Background of vibrant `#10E868` filled with `#0F172A` text, weighted at `600`. On hover, elevates with an electric lime glow shadow (`0px 8px 24px rgba(16, 232, 104, 0.45)`) and a `-1px` vertical lift.
- **Gradient Action**: Styled with `gradient-bitree-signature` text fill on white surfaces, or full gradient background with crisp white text.
- **Secondary Ghost**: Pure white background, `1.5px` border in `#0F172A`, text in `#0F172A`. Transitions on hover to an inverted `#0F172A` fill with white text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags**: Capsule shaped (`rounded-full`) with a soft tinted background (e.g., `rgba(16, 232, 104, 0.12)`) and saturated high-contrast label color (`#047857`).
- **Studio Gradient Badges**: 1px gradient border running from electric lime to cyan with uppercase `label-sm` tracking.

### Cards & Surfaces
- **Studio Showcase Card**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with an ultra-thin `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. On hover, the border transitions to a vibrant gradient stroke (`#10E868` to `#0284C7`), paired with an elevated ambient drop shadow.
- **Feature Card**: Soft surface tint of `#F8FAFC` accented with a 3px neon colored left bar or radial glow backdrop peeking behind the corner.

### Form Inputs
- **Input Fields**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface, `1.5px` solid border in `#CBD5E1`, text in `#0F172A`. On focus, transitions to `#0284C7` or `#10E868` with a 3px soft focus ring (`rgba(16, 232, 104, 0.25)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom circular and squircle controls filled with `#10E868` when checked, featuring white checkmark glyphs and high-contrast borders.

### Interactive Micro-Elements
- **Creative Cursor Trackers**: Soft trailing neon blur in lime green and cyan that gently illuminates underlying cards.
- **Progress & Metrics Indicators**: Vivid multi-color linear bars utilizing the studio signature gradient across analytics and creative deliverable status bars.
